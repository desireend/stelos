About

Organization overview

Stelos is a volunteer 3D printing initiative that turns community needs into durable, practical items. Each project begins with a partner organization identifying a specific need among the people it serves. We then design or adapt a model, test it, and print and deliver the finished items free of charge.

The initiative was founded by a former president of a National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) collegiate chapter, and it reflects NSBE's commitment to engineers who positively impact their community. We are working toward registration as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

04
Programs

Program model

Phase 1

Needs assessment

Partner organizations identify the specific needs of the people they serve.

Phase 2

Design and production

Models are designed, tested and printed in the material best suited to daily use.

Phase 3

Delivery and reporting

Items are delivered at no cost, and every item and recipient is recorded.

PETG is used for assistive devices that are gripped and twisted daily, where toughness and resistance to cracking matter most.
PLA is used for children's learning tools and shelter items, where clean finishes and reliable printing matter most.

Sponsorship

Sponsorship opportunities

Filament is the primary constraint on how many people we can serve. Sponsors supply the material behind every item we deliver, and receive recognition and regular impact reporting in return. Our current goal is 21 spools of black and white 1.75 mm PLA or PETG by November 30, 2026, followed by ongoing support for K–12 outreach at the Dallas JCC.

  • Delivery records confirmed with each partner
  • Print logs tracking material use and success rate
  • Items printed and people served, reported quarterly
